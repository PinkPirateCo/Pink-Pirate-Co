Island Luxury Co.™

Visual Identity + Product Packaging

Island Luxury Co. is a brand I’ve been part of since day one. I led the creative direction from the ground up - developing the logo, color palette, visual identity, and full packaging system. The founder had a strong vision for the vibe: tropical, elevated, and transportive. My role was to bring that feeling to life in a way that felt cohesive, premium, and shelf-ready.

In-Store Visual Impact

These oversized window clings were designed to turn ordinary fridge doors into full-on brand billboards. Bold claims. Lush tropical visuals. Zero chance of being ignored.

They amplify key benefits, command attention in crowded retail environments, and create a cohesive island experience that pulls shoppers in before they even reach for the can.
