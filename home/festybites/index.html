FestyBites®

Visual identity + product packaging

FestyBites is a dynamic player in the pet industry specializing in dog supplements. With a focus on embodying vitality and trust, I crafted a visual identity and packaging that seamlessly merged boldness, brightness, playfulness, and modernity. Drawing inspiration from the energetic spirit of canine companionship, the visual elements exude a playful charm, inviting customers to engage with the brand on an emotional level. From the sleek logo design to the dynamic graphics adorning the packaging, every aspect was meticulously crafted to resonate with the target audience and leave a lasting impression.
