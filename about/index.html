FAQs

How much will it cost?

Because each project is unique there is no one price that “fits all”. By answering the questions it gives us a better understanding on how to properly quote your project and get a grasp for the scope of work involved.

How many concepts do you provide?

We’ll present you with 2-3 concepts. You'll have the freedom to mix and match different elements/ideas that you'd like to see for the next round (max. 2 revisions).

Do you offer printing services?

No, we do not provide printing services directly, but we can recommend reputable companies.

What is included in a brand identity package?

- Main logo
- Secondary logo
- Social media logo
- Color palette
- Brand guide
- Social media covers
- Typography
- Business cards
- Social media templates

What's included in a logo package?

- Main logo
- Secondary logo
- Social media icon
- Color palette
- Style guide

How do you accept payments?

We use PayPal for payments

How will I receive my design project?

Once all artwork is finalized, you’ll receive your deliverables via your own private dropbox link.
